# Product Roadmap

**Project:** VEL Finance  
**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** July 2026

---

# Purpose

This roadmap outlines the planned evolution of the VEL Finance application beyond its initial production release.

It provides a structured vision for future development by organizing planned enhancements into phased releases. The roadmap balances business needs, technical improvements, and user experience enhancements while maintaining a stable and maintainable architecture.

---

# Vision

The long-term vision of VEL Finance is to become a reliable, scalable, and user-friendly finance management platform for small and medium-sized lending businesses.

The application will continue to evolve while preserving the core principles established in Version 1.0:

- Simple and intuitive user experience
- Backend-owned business logic
- Reliable financial calculations
- Modular architecture
- Scalable system design

# Development Principles

Future development will continue to follow these principles:

- Maintain a modular architecture.
- Keep business logic centralized in the backend.
- Prioritize backward compatibility.
- Preserve data integrity.
- Focus on user experience.
- Ensure scalability and maintainability.
